I'm interested in condensed matter theory and geometry hidden in such quantum systems.

My research projects are:

  • Extending orbital Wigner function formalism to magnetic fields to study quantum fermionic transport in multiband systems.
  • Non-invertible symmetries, gauging, domain wall condensation in non-abelian G-Qudit chains.
  • (Formerly) Vanadium dioxide spin Seebeck effects.
  • (Formerly) Why donut shaped soap bubbles don't exist?

Beyond research, I am passionate about creative physics problems and participated in Asian & International Physics Olympiad in 2023. I grew up in Chittagong.

  • Prof. Douglas Natelson - vanadium spin transport
